MODULE FORGE — MODULE BUILDER

Build custom upgrade modules. Add one or more effects (mix buffs and debuffs freely). Copy the JSON or save it into your modules folder, then restart the game.

Basics

Availability

PAY WITH INGREDIENTS AS WELL AS MONEY. The five below are the ones the game actually trades in, and they stack on top of shopPrice — which is what every one of the 62 stock shop items does. For an ingredient-only cost set shopPrice to 0. The shop draws one icon per currency by itself and reddens the ones you cannot afford, so this needs no UI of its own.

Two of the names differ from what the game stores them under — Fiber is Coral and Generator is Shell — so either spelling is accepted. Hand-edited files may also name any ship resource (Tech, Purple…), and the four ingredients nothing in the game drops (Bond, Ex, Face, Strange Ball), which are warned about rather than refused.

Whole units only. A cost below 1 is dropped and said so: the game's purchase code subtracts without checking the ship can hold the currency, and a zero slips past the affordability check that would otherwise stop it.

Buff indicator — where the settings are

Effects

Effects for the selected target. Mix positive and negative freely (e.g. +1 projectile and -20% fire rate).

EVERY EFFECT BELOW NEEDS THE CARD TO BE CONNECTED AND POWERED, exactly as the game's own modules do — a card lying loose in the grid, or connected but outside its cluster's power core, does nothing at all, and starts working the moment you plug it in. The grid already shows you which is which: an unpowered or unconnected icon is drawn differently, and the game's own hover card treats such a module as not installed. Until 2026-09-11 Module Forge did not honour that — an effect registered itself when the card was placed and never checked again, so an unpowered module kept paying out. If one of yours ever appears to do nothing, check the power before you check the file, and look in the log for the line naming the card.
